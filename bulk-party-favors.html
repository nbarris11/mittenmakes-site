Bulk 3D Printed Party Favors Made to Order

Mitten Makes makes custom 3D printed party favors in quantities from 10 to 50 or more — for birthday party bags, classroom sets, teacher appreciation events, and any occasion where you need a lot of something small and thoughtful. Every piece is made to order in Farmington Hills, MI and can be color-matched or personalized for the occasion.

What works well for bulk orders

The best bulk party favor pieces are compact, durable, and easy to customize. These are the most popular options for quantity orders at Mitten Makes:

  • Articulated animal keychains — Penguins, elephants, turtles, and other creatures that work on backpacks, keyrings, and party bags. Available in any color.
  • Fidgets — Star fidgets, flexi stars, and slime fidgets are popular birthday bag picks for kids of all ages. Simple, satisfying, and easy to hand off.
  • Mini creatures — Tiny T-Rexes, cookie dragons, and other small articulated figures that feel collectible and fun without being fragile.
  • Bookmarks — A practical pick for classroom events, reading celebrations, and school-themed parties. Can be made in school colors.
  • Custom name pieces — If each guest gets their own name on the favor, personalized keychains and small signs are the most popular option.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for bulk party favors?

There is no strict minimum — single pieces are always welcome. Bulk pricing and color-matching typically makes the most sense starting at 10 pieces, and most birthday and classroom orders fall in the 15 to 30 range.

Can each piece have a different name or color?

Yes. Name personalization and per-piece color variation are both available. Just mention it in your request and we'll confirm the best approach for your order size.

How far in advance should I order bulk party favors?

For quantities of 25 or more, ordering 2 to 3 weeks ahead is recommended. Smaller sets of 10 to 15 pieces can often be ready within 5 to 7 business days. Rush requests are sometimes possible — ask and we'll let you know.

Do you ship bulk orders or is it pickup only?

Both. Local pickup in Farmington Hills is always available and has no minimum. Shipped bulk orders go anywhere in the U.S. Shipping cost is confirmed when we finalize your order details.

Can I see a sample before the full order is printed?

Yes — for larger orders, a sample piece can be arranged before the full quantity is printed. Ask about this when you send your initial request.
