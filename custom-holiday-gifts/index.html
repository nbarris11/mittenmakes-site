Holiday order questions

When do I need to order for Christmas?

For shipped orders, order by Tuesday, December 8. For free local pickup in Farmington Hills, order by Monday, December 14. Custom pieces with names, photos, or logos can take longer than our usual 5 to 7 business days, so earlier is better.

When do I need to order for Hanukkah?

Hanukkah starts the evening of December 4 this year. Order by Monday, November 23 to ship, or Wednesday, November 25 for local pickup so your gift is ready for the first night.

Should I pick up or have it shipped?

Local pickup in Farmington Hills is free, and it is the fastest option for last-minute gifts. U.S. shipping is $10, or free on orders over $49.97. Shipping adds a few days in December, so pickup gives you the latest order-by date.

Can you match a photo, logo, or name?

Yes. Send us the name, photo, or logo with your order and we will tell you what will print well. Lithophanes and photo lightboxes are made from a photo, and name signs, keychains, and team pieces can use a name or a simple logo.

Is there a minimum for bulk holiday orders?

There's no minimum, and orders of 10 or more get quantity pricing. Tell us how many you need and your date, and we will confirm pricing before anything is due.

Do you offer gift wrap?

We don't offer gift wrap, but everything is packed so it's easy to wrap yourself.

How do I pay?

We confirm the final price before anything is due. Pickup orders can pay with cash or Venmo. Shipped orders can pay with Venmo or PayPal. Items in the online shop can also be paid by card at checkout.
