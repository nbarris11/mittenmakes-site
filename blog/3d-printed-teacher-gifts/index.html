Common questions about 3D printed teacher gifts

How much should I spend on a teacher gift?

For an individual thank-you gift, most 3D printed pieces from Mitten Makes fall between $8 and $22 — a comfortable range for a thoughtful, handmade item. For classroom sets or grade-level orders, pricing scales with quantity and is always confirmed before anything is due.

What teacher gifts work best for a whole grade?

Keychains, bookmarks, and small fidgets are the most practical options for larger classroom or grade-level orders. They're affordable per piece, easy to customize with a school color or theme, and light enough that teachers can carry them home without any trouble.

Can each gift have a different teacher's name on it?

Yes. Name personalization is available on most pieces. For classroom sets where each piece needs a different name, just include the name list when you send the request and we'll confirm the best approach for your order.

How far in advance should I order teacher gifts?

For individual gifts, ordering 1 to 2 weeks ahead is comfortable. For classroom sets of 20 or more pieces, 2 to 3 weeks is recommended — especially around teacher appreciation week in May when orders tend to stack up.

Do you ship teacher gifts or is pickup only?

Both. Local pickup in Farmington Hills is always welcome and has no minimum. Shipped orders are available anywhere in the U.S. with a flat $9.97 shipping fee on orders over $24.97.
