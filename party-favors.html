Events these work well for

Party favors are not just for one kind of celebration.

Kids’ birthdays

We create party favors for kids’ birthdays, especially when the goal is to match a theme or hand out something more fun than the usual bag filler.

School and classroom events

Party favors can also work well for classroom celebrations, school events, and small giftable pieces for larger groups.

Special occasions and showers

These kinds of small custom pieces can also make sense for themed celebrations, family events, and other occasions where you want a more memorable takeaway.

Popular themes and favor types

Some styles show up again and again because they are easy to gift and easy to hand out.

Dinosaurs, creatures, and fidgets

Dinosaurs, creatures, 3D printed fidget toys, and other playful designs are popular choices, especially for birthday bags and themed celebrations.

Names and personalized details

Party favors can often be customized with names, colors, or a style that fits your event, depending on the design and quantity.

Simple mini gifts

The best favor is usually the one that feels fun without being complicated. Smaller articulated animals and other easy-to-hand-out pieces are usually a strong place to start.
