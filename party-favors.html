How party favor ordering works

Simple process, no guesswork.

Tell us the basics

Party date, how many guests, age group, and any theme or color ideas you have. You do not need a finished design. A rough idea is enough to get started.

We confirm the details

We will follow up with options, pricing per piece, total cost, and an estimated ready date. Nothing is due until you approve the plan.

We print and deliver

Once confirmed, we print the full set fresh in Metro Detroit. Local pickup in Farmington Hills or shipped anywhere in the U.S. We recommend reaching out at least 2 to 3 weeks before your party date for best results.

A few things worth knowing

No minimum quantity

Whether you need 8 or 80, we can work with you on the right size order. Larger quantities just need a little more lead time.

Plan ahead for best results

Favor sets of 10 or more take longer than a single item. We recommend reaching out 2 to 3 weeks before your party date. Rush requests may be possible depending on timing, so ask.

Colors and names are flexible

Each piece in the set can share a color theme, or you can mix colors across the set. Names on each piece are also an option for smaller quantities.

No payment until confirmed

We confirm pricing, timing, and delivery before anything is due. Local pickup pays at pickup by cash or Venmo. Shipped orders pay by Venmo or PayPal after confirmation.
